# Three-minute demo script

1. Open the home screen on a phone, switch between Italian and English, and highlight the installable PWA experience with no app-store dependency.
2. Select **Precompila demo**. Explain that it speeds up the presentation while leaving every field editable.
3. Walk through the five steps: guided UX, validation, evidence photos, and the legal disclaimer.
4. Submit the claim and show the unique reference and the priority generated by the triage rules.
5. Open the Kafka console and show `ClaimSubmitted` on the `claim-events` topic.
6. Select a date, loss adjuster, and available slot, then confirm the appointment.
7. Show `AppointmentBooked` and close with the transactional outbox and Elasticsearch projection as production-oriented evolutions.

## Suggested cover message

> I turned your technology stack into a small end-to-end insurance flow: a React PWA for completing a digital accident report, backed by Kotlin and Quarkus with MongoDB persistence and Kafka events that decouple claim submission from downstream processes. I deliberately kept the domain small but fully executable, and documented the trade-offs I would address when taking it to production.
